Diwali

🎆✨ Get Ready for CBIA Diwali Festival 2026! ✨🎆

🪔 Celebrate the Festival of Lights with the Coastal Bend India Association (CBIA)! 🪔
Join us for a spectacular evening filled with Indian culture, delicious food, dazzling performances, and festive fun with family and friends!

📅 Date: Saturday, November 14, 2026
🕔 Time: 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM
📍 Venue: W.B. Ray High School
1002 Texan Trail
Corpus Christi, TX 78411

🎉 What’s Happening?

🥳 5:00 – 6:00 PM: Registration, Photo Booth, Appetizers & Chai
💃 6:00 – 7:30 PM: Cultural Program featuring amazing performances by our talented community members
🍛 7:30 – 9:00 PM: Delicious Indian Dinner with family and friends

  • Gobi Machurian
  • Chicken 65
  • Paneer Tikka Masala
  • Navratan Korma
  • Hydrabadi Murg Masala
  • Chicken Biryani with Salan
  • Riata
  • Papad
  • Rasmalai
🍽️ Freshly Prepared Food!

This year, our dedicated food committee is bringing you an even better dining experience! Selected food items, including appetizers and fresh puris, will be cooked live at the venue and served hot on your plates. Come hungry and enjoy authentic Indian flavors!

🎟️ Diwali Festival Tickets

⭐ CBIA Members – Early Bird: $35 per person

(Tickets purchased before November 12, 2026)

⭐ CBIA Members – Late Bird: $40 per person

(Tickets purchased on or after November 12, 2026)

⭐ Non-Members: $50 per person

👧👦 Children under 10 years: FREE!
